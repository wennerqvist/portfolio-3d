02 About

Creative
Developer

I'm Jakob Wennerqvist — a creative developer based in Stockholm, working where design and code overlap. I build interactive experiences for the web: 3D scenes, generative visuals, and interfaces that feel alive.

Over the past years I've worked across brand, motion and web — from real-time installations shown at festivals to identity systems for small studios. I care about the details: easing curves, typography, and the last 10% that makes a thing feel finished.

When I'm not shipping, I'm experimenting with shaders, audio-reactive visuals, and whatever the browser lets me get away with.

Capabilities

  • Three.js / WebGL
  • GSAP & motion design
  • Creative frontend (JS / TS)
  • Generative & audio-reactive visuals
  • Brand & identity
  • UI / interaction design
