I'm Jakob

I'm Jakob Wennerqvist, a digital marketer who gets excited about solving problems with strategy, creativity, and a little AI magic.

I've always been someone who challenges myself. I moved across the Atlantic to study in Chicago, then back again to pursue my MBA in Rome without speaking the language fluently (still don't, but we're working on it). I published a book at 21 because I saw a real problem in vision screening and wanted to do something about it. And right now? I'm challenging myself to figure out how AI can make marketing smarter, specifically in automation and personalization.

My sweet spot is where brand strategy meets data. I love building campaigns that actually move the needle, understanding what the numbers are telling you, and figuring out how to scale impact without losing the human touch. Whether it's repositioning a company, analyzing what works, or thinking three steps ahead, that's where I come alive.

I'm currently hunting for my next marketing role where I can put these skills to work and explore how AI can amplify what good marketers do.

If you think we could build something interesting together, or just want to chat about marketing, AI, or life adventures, hit me up on LinkedIn. I promise to write something more interesting than “let's connect.” 😊
